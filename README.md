## 🧠 The Project
**HCAE** is a multi-tiered Prompt Engineering framework designed to bypass AI Detectors through architectural subversion. Traditional spinners swap words but leave the AI's mathematical DNA intact. This project forces LLMs to alter their fundamental probabilistic generation.

As AI detectors have evolved from basic n-gram analysis to deep syntactic evaluation, and now to **anomaly detection** (flagging text that is "too perfectly asymmetrical"), this repository contains three generations of bypass methodologies, each targeting a deeper layer of the detection stack.

---

## 📂 The Framework Tiers

### Tier 1: Cognitive Asymmetry Engine (Baseline)
**File:** [`Prompt-v1.md`](./Prompt-v1.md)

Targets foundational metrics: **Perplexity** (word predictability) and **Burstiness** (sentence length variation). 

*   **Mechanism:** Forces sub-optimal vocabulary, destroys SVO rhythm, and injects conversational transitions.
*   **Best for:** General content, blogs, SEO, and older detection systems.

---

### Tier 2: Syntactic Topology Subversion (Advanced)
**File:** [`Prompt-v2.md`](./Prompt-v2.md)

Reverse-engineered to defeat next-gen detectors (GPTZero v4+, Turnitin) by attacking the **structural logic** of generation.

*   **Mechanism:** Breaks symmetrical syntax trees, eradicates lexical cohesion (stops repeating root words), and clusters information density (cramming facts into one sentence, followed by a low-info conclusion).
*   **Best for:** Strict academic writing, technical documentation, and aggressive modern detectors.

---

### Tier 3: Cognitive Entropy & Metacognitive Drafting (God Tier)
**File:** [`Prompt-v3.md`](./Prompt-v3.md)

Developed to defeat the newest frontier of detection: **Anomaly Detection on Forced Humanization**. Detectors now flag text that is *too consistently asymmetric*. Tier 3 stops treating the output as text, and starts simulating the human act of *thinking and drafting*.

*   **Mechanism:** 
    *   **Pragmatic Ellipsis:** Deliberately omits "obvious" conclusions that AI normally over-explains.
    *   **Micro-Tangential Drift:** Breaks linear A->B->C logic by inserting tiny, irrelevant observations before moving to the next point.
    *   **Punctuation Entropy:** Uses em-dashes and semicolons not for grammar, but to simulate the rhythm of human thought and breathing.
    *   **Epistemic Hedging:** Replaces AI certainty with experiential, idiosyncratic human uncertainty ("What this seems to suggest is...").
    *   **The Anti-Gloss:** Deliberately chooses "clunkier" phrasing to avoid the AI's obsession with elegant metaphors.
*   **Best for:** High-stakes academic publishing, professional essays, and bypassing AI detectors that look for "AI trying to fake human imperfection."

## 🚀 Why Not Use Traditional Spinners?

Tools like Quillbot swap synonyms but leave the AI's **syntactic skeleton** untouched. If the sentence structure is mathematically symmetrical, it is flagged as AI. HCAE forces the LLM to generate deformed, asymmetric, and cognitively authentic skeletons from the first token.

5. Paste the AI-generated text you want to rewrite immediately after the prompt.
